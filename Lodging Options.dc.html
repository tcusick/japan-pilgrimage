Japan Pilgrimage · Lodging Options
Part two · March 12–23, 2027

Lodging options

Chikatsuyu, a Hongu arrival, a river boat to Shingu, a Nachi finish and Ise. Two to four options for each night, with price, onsen, vegetarian handling, and what counts for and against. Town descriptions come from listings and general knowledge; confirm opening hours and prices before you go. Prices are US dollars per person, for one traveler with dinner and breakfast, from Kumano Travel and other listings viewed in November 2026. Rates for March 2027, onsen tax (about $1) and holiday surcharges need checking at booking.

Fri Mar 12

Kii-Tanabe

1 night · arrival, positioning

This night is a base for the morning bus to Takijiri-oji. Hotel Kii Tanabe is the pick and Django is the backup. Kamenoi Hotel Kii-Tanabe (coastal onsen hotel, 11 min drive from the station, vegetarian OK on Kumano Travel, about $88–105 with dinner) is the onsen alternative if you want one. Trail start and end: Mar 13 start: bus from Kii-Tanabe Station to Takijiri-oji, about 40 min.

The town: A working city and the gateway to the trail, with restaurants, shops, a tourist information office and a convenience store by the station. Nearby on foot: Tokei Shrine, Kozan-ji temple and Ogigahama beach (about 15 min). Many shops close early.

Sat Mar 13

Takahara

1 night · Takijiri-oji → Takahara, 2.3 mi

The town: A ridge-top hamlet at about 300 m with around 70 residents, terraced rice fields and mountain views. There is Takahara Kumano Shrine, a rest area with a sea-of-clouds view in the morning, and good stargazing. Beyond the inns there is nothing else, and one café on the trail runs only on limited days.

Sun Mar 14

Chikatsuyu

1 night · Takahara → Chikatsuyu, about 6.0 mi

Mar 14 is a shorter 6.0 mi walk from Takahara. The walk to Tsugizakura-oji and the transfer to Hosshinmon-oji come on Mar 15. Chikatsuyu is a village on the Tanabe to Hongu bus line, with the Nakahechi Art Museum, Chikatsuyu-oji and a Coop store. Aside from the inns, the Coop is the only place in the settlement to buy food, so take a lunch box. Minshuku Nakano, another family inn in the south of the village, is rated 4.19 from 629 reviews on Kumano Travel; I did not find its prices. Prices are per person.

The town: A small village on the Hiki River with Chikatsuyu-oji, the Nakahechi Art Museum, the Hisui-no-yu onsen ($4 for non-guests) and a Coop store. It was a post town on the old pilgrim route. A few hours is enough.

Mon–Tue Mar 15–16

Yunomine Onsen

2 nights · Hongu on arrival, rest day

Ryokan Adumaya is the pick for both nights and Minshuku Yunosato is the backup. Trail start and end: Mar 15 ends at Hongu Taisha and Oyunohara. The Hongu to Yunomine bus is about 10 min. Mar 16 is a rest day in Yunomine.

The town: A small valley village of inns and one of the oldest hot springs in Japan. It has Tsuboyu, the World Heritage bath, and a hot-spring stream running through the village. Kumano Hongu Taisha and its Heritage Center are a bus ride away (about 10 min, $2), as is Kawayu Onsen with its riverbed hot spring. Restaurants are few, so dinner is at the inn.

Nachisan, then Kii-Katsuura

2 nights · Nachisan Mar 18, Kii-Katsuura onsen day Mar 19

Mar 18 is on Nachisan: Mitaki Sanso is the only lodging on the mountain, and Guesthouse Michi is a private house nearby. Mar 19 is an onsen day in Kii-Katsuura: Kamenoi is the pick, Nakanoshima the island splurge, Hotel Urashima has the cave baths, and Kosakaya and Wakatake are simpler onsen minshuku. Simpler places come first: Kosakaya and Wakatake. Also listed near the station on Kumano Travel: Oyado Hana Guesthouse, a converted older building, and JIYUJIYA, a self-check-in guesthouse opened by Wakatake’s owner. I have not read their details. Trail start and end: Mar 18 ends at Nachi Taisha. Bus from Nachisan to Kii-Katsuura Station is about 25 min.

The town: A coastal town. It has hot-spring hotels and a train station. Nachi Falls, Kumano Nachi Taisha and the Daimon-zaka path are about 20–25 min by bus ($3–630).
