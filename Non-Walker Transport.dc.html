Japan Pilgrimage · Non-walker transport
March 12–15 · two travelers not walking

Getting from Kii-Tanabe to Yunomine

Three legs: Kii-Tanabe to Takahara, Takahara to Chikatsuyu, and Chikatsuyu to Yunomine. A taxi is a private car for up to four, so for two people the public bus is likely cheaper, though I could not confirm bus fares. Prices are US dollars at ¥150 to $1, with yen beneath.

Taxi and luggage prices come from KUMANO TRAVEL listings. I could not confirm 2027 bus times or fares, so the bus details below are partial. Check them with the Tanabe tourism bureau before booking.

Mar 13

Kii-Tanabe to Takahara

Recommended
Ryujin bus to Takijiri, then Komorian shuttle
Daily buses run from Kii-Tanabe Station to the Takijiri stop. One listed departure is 13:00 from Kii-Tanabe, reaching Takijiri at 13:41, from a 2024 tour page. Other departures and the fare are not confirmed. Komorian's shuttle is free from Takijiri, Takahara or Kurisugawa, so ask Miko to meet the bus at Takijiri.
Taxi
Meiko Taxi, door to door · $93
¥13,900 per vehicle from Kii-Tanabe Station to Takahara, so about $46 each for two. A normal taxi holds 4 people and 2 suitcases. To Takijiri only it is ¥10,900 ($73). Book through KUMANO TRAVEL.
Mar 14

Takahara to Chikatsuyu

Ask first
Komorian's shuttle to Kurisugawa, then the bus
Ask Miko whether the shuttle can take them down to the Kurisugawa bus stop, or all the way to Chikatsuyu. I found no confirmed bus time or fare for this leg. Ask Miko for the bus time as well.
Taxi
Short taxi ride · price not found
For scale, Kii-Tanabe to Takahara is ¥13,900 and to Chikatsuyu ¥17,300, so the Takahara to Chikatsuyu leg is likely a few thousand yen. That is my estimate, not a quote. Ask Meiko or Shirahama Daiichi Taxi for the actual price.
Mar 15

Chikatsuyu to Yunomine

Recommended
Ryujin bus to Yunomine Onsen
Local buses connect Kii-Tanabe to the Hongu area, including Yunomine Onsen, along the route past Chikatsuyu. I could not confirm a Chikatsuyu departure time or fare. Ask the Tanabe tourism bureau or Adumaya for the timetable.
Taxi
Taxi to Yunomine · price not found
For scale, Kii-Tanabe to the Hongu area is ¥30,700 ($205) per normal taxi. Ask for a Chikatsuyu to Yunomine quote. Useful if they want a later start or have more bags than the bus allows.

Luggage

Buses are easier with only a small bag each. Shuttle prices are per leg, per group, from KUMANO TRAVEL listings,.

Tanabe to Takijiri or Takahara (Kumo shuttle)from $32 (¥4,800)
Tanabe to Chikatsuyu or Nonakafrom $41 (¥6,100)
Chikatsuyu or Nonaka to the Hongu areafrom $24 (¥3,600)
Tanabe to the Hongu area, same dayfrom $57 (¥8,500)

Shuttles need booking at least 10 days ahead, collect from lodging booked on KUMANO TRAVEL, and take pickups until about 10:00. Komorian is not a KUMANO TRAVEL booking, so ask it about luggage hand-off.

What I would do

  • Mar 13: bus to Takijiri and the free Komorian shuttle. Book a taxi only as a fallback.
  • Mar 14: ask Miko to help first. If she cannot, a short taxi.
  • Mar 15: the Ryujin bus from Chikatsuyu to Yunomine, with luggage shuttled ahead to Adumaya.
  • Get the 2027 bus times from the Tanabe tourism bureau. Book a taxi for one leg only if a bus time doesn't fit.
