March 12–20 · planning page, including non-walker options

People and luggage plan

Three people walk and two do not. The walkers carry day packs, and the non-walkers should not be tied to the bags. Bus, taxi, shuttle and takkyubin times and prices come from earlier research and are not confirmed for 2027. Lines on the map are schematic.

Day by day

Big bags go from Kyoto to Osaka and are not needed again, so each person keeps one backpack or carry-on size bag.

Mar 12
Kyoto to Kii-Tanabe
Hotel Kii Tanabe
People
Everyone by rail.
Bags
Ask the hotel to hold bags after check-out and, if needed, ship them to Komorian for Mar 13.
Mar 13
Kii-Tanabe to Takahara
Komorian (booked)
People
Miko’s plan. Walkers take the 11:35 a.m. bus to Takijiri and walk 2.3 mi to Takahara, arriving about 3:00 p.m. Non-walkers take the 1:00 p.m. bus to Takijiri and visit Takijiri-oji and the Kumano Kodo Kan. Miko picks them up shortly after 2:00 p.m., collects the walkers at Takahara, and drives everyone to Komorian.
Bags · decision 1
Mahoroma, Kii-Tanabe to Takijiri/Takahara: $33 for up to 2 pieces, +$7 for each extra piece.
Mar 14
Takahara to Chikatsuyu
Minshuku Wago
People
Walkers walk 6.0 mi. If the non-walkers also skip the walk, Miko drops them at the Kurisugawa bus stop, and they take the bus to Chikatsuyu.
Bags · decision 2
Mahoroma, Takahara to Chikatsuyu: $23 for up to 2 pieces, plus $7 for each extra piece. Ask Wago to hold the bags if the non-walkers arrive early.
Mar 15–16
Chikatsuyu to Yunomine
Ryokan Adumaya, 2 nights
People
Walkers go by Tsugizakura-oji, bus to Hosshinmon-oji, then Hongu, 6.1 mi. Non-walkers take the bus to Yunomine. Mar 16 is a rest day.
Bags
Mahoroma, Chikatsuyu to the Hongu area (includes Yunomine): $23 for up to 2 pieces, plus $7 for each extra piece. Adumaya holds bags before check-in.
Mar 17
Yunomine to Shingu
Guest House No-Che (booked)
People
Everyone takes the bus to the Kawabune Center and the 2:30 p.m. river boat (booked, 5 adults), arriving about 4:00 p.m.
Bags · decision 3
Mahoroma, Hongu area to Shingu: $27 for up to 2 pieces, plus $7 for each extra piece, so the bags do not go on the boat. Ask the Shingu host about receiving them.
Mar 18–19
Shingu to Nachisan, then Kii-Katsuura
Mitaki Sanso, then Kamenoi Hotel Nachi Katsuura
People
Train from Shingu to Kii-Katsuura, then the bus to Nachisan on Mar 18. On Mar 19 the bus back down, about 25 min.
Bags · decision 4
Either carry the bags on the train and bus, or use Mahoroma, Shingu to Nachi-Katsuura ($37 for up to 2 pieces, plus $7 for each extra piece). Ask whether it delivers to Mitaki Sanso on Nachisan, and whether Nachisan to Kii-Katsuura is covered.
Mar 20
Kii-Katsuura to Ise
Ise house (booked), 4 people
People
Rail along the coast, with optional stops on the way.
Bags
Carry on the train or ship to the Ise house. Use station lockers on any stop.
